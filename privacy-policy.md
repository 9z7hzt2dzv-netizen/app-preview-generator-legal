# Privacy Policy

**AppreviewGen**
Last updated: 4 September 2026

## The short version

**AppreviewGen collects nothing.**

The app has no network code in it. It cannot send anything anywhere, because there is
nothing in it that opens a connection. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics,
no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no tracking of any kind.

Everything the app does — laying out a screenshot, rendering it, encoding a video — happens
on your device.

## Your screenshots and recordings

The images and screen recordings you import are **copied into the app's own folder on your
device and go nowhere else**. They are not uploaded, not backed up to any service run by
the developer, and never seen by anyone but you.

What you export is written where you send it: a folder you choose, or your photo library.
Nothing is written anywhere you did not ask for.

## What the app stores on your device

Two things, both inside the app's sandbox:

**Your projects.** A folder per project under the app's Application Support directory,
holding a `project.json` describing the layout and an `Assets` folder holding the media you
imported.

**A small set of preferences**, kept under a single key in the standard preferences store.
Specifically: which project and frame you had open, which inspector page was showing, the
tint, device and finish you last chose, the compression setting you last used on import,
the export sizes and target you last ticked, how the library was last sorted and packed,
whether the last export went to your photo library, and a bookmark to the folder you last
exported to.

That last item is a *security-scoped bookmark* — the system's own record of the permission
you granted when you picked that folder. It is what lets the app write there again after a
relaunch. It identifies a folder on your Mac, not you.

None of it identifies you. None of it leaves the device. All of it is deleted when you
delete the app.

## Photos

The app asks for **add-only** access to your photo library, and only when you export to it.
Add-only means exactly that: it can save a finished screenshot or video, and it cannot read,
list or search anything already in there.

Importing from your photo library goes through the system photo picker, which hands the app
only the items you pick and needs no permission at all.

## What the app does not do

- It does not use the network, at all.
- It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, health data, or
  any other sensor or personal database.
- It does not read your photo library.
- It does not use the device advertising identifier, and it does not ask for tracking
  permission, because it does not track.
- It does not contain analytics, crash-reporting or attribution frameworks.
- It does not show advertising.
- It does not contain in-app purchases or subscriptions.

## Purchases

AppreviewGen is a paid app bought from the App Store. The purchase is handled entirely by
Apple. We never see your payment details, your Apple Account, or your name. Apple may
provide the developer with aggregate, anonymous sales reports, subject to your own App Store
privacy settings, which you control in Settings on your device.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, of any age, which means it collects none from
children either.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version is published at this address and the date
at the top changes with it. Because the app collects nothing, no change here can
retroactively affect data that was never gathered.
